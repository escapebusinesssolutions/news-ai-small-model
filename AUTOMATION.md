# Stage 8 — Automate

## Objective
Turn proven commercial decisions into bounded automated decisions without removing human accountability for consequential changes.

**Rule: automate repeatable decisions, not uncertainty.**

## Automation loop
1. Collect audience and commercial performance data.
2. Identify winners and bottlenecks.
3. Rank candidate opportunities.
4. Select a bounded production batch.
5. Generate and validate content using existing controls.
6. Publish only when validation passes.
7. Measure results.
8. Feed evidence back into prioritization.

## Automatable decisions
- Topic prioritization from measured demand.
- Selection of existing winning clusters for additional coverage.
- Selection of catalogue-supported products.
- Internal-link opportunities.
- Production scheduling within approved limits.
- Reporting and anomaly detection.

## Guardrails
- Never auto-invent products, facts, reviews, prices, specifications, compatibility, rankings, or testing.
- Never bypass affiliate validation.
- Never bypass publication validation.
- Never increase production volume without a defined limit.
- Never treat traffic alone as commercial success.
- Never automatically enter a new marketplace, affiliate program, or materially new vertical without explicit approval.

## Human approval boundary
Human review remains appropriate for:
- New revenue sources.
- New marketplaces/accounts.
- Major category expansion.
- Changes to editorial standards.
- Material infrastructure spend.
- Brand/legal/compliance decisions.

## Success definition
The system can continuously discover, prioritize, produce, validate, publish, and measure commercially relevant content within explicit operating boundaries.
