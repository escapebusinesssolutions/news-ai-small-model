# Stage 10 — Business Scale

## Objective
Scale TechSignal as a business only when the economics justify additional resources.

**Rule: capital follows proven returns.**

## Scale gates
- Repeatable traffic acquisition exists.
- Repeatable commercial conversion exists.
- Unit economics are measurable.
- Quality remains stable at higher volume.
- The bottleneck is understood.
- Additional resources have a credible path to incremental profit.

## Core economics
Track:
- Revenue per visitor.
- Revenue per 1,000 visitors.
- Revenue per article.
- Affiliate CTR.
- Conversion rate where measurable.
- Earnings by product, topic, and traffic source.
- Production cost/time.
- Infrastructure and API cost.
- Contribution margin.

## Capital allocation
Prioritize incremental resources in this order:
1. Fix the largest proven commercial bottleneck.
2. Increase production around profitable winners.
3. Improve distribution with measurable return.
4. Improve proprietary data/evidence where it strengthens economics.
5. Add infrastructure only when it removes a demonstrated constraint.
6. Add people only when automation or capital cannot economically solve the constraint.

## Scale / hold / kill
### Scale
Use when incremental output produces repeatable incremental economic value.

### Hold
Use when evidence is promising but sample size or repeatability is insufficient.

### Kill or pivot
Use when sustained effort produces weak commercial economics and no credible path to improvement.

## Spend discipline
Do not introduce recurring costs simply because paid infrastructure is available. Every material cost should have an identified bottleneck and measurable expected benefit.

## Business options after proof
If economics become strong, evaluate:
- More categories.
- More marketplaces/affiliate programs.
- First-party audience products.
- Sponsorships or direct commercial relationships.
- Multiple specialized properties.
- Human editorial/testing capacity.
- Potential strategic partnership or sale.

These are options, not assumptions.

## Success definition
TechSignal becomes a repeatable, measurable business in which additional capital, automation, or people can be allocated against known constraints with an expected economic return.
