# Stage 5 — Scale

## Objective
Increase TechSignal output and revenue only after the commercial loop produces evidence that scaling is justified.

**Rule: scale what works; do not scale page count for its own sake.**

Google's current Search guidance warns that generating many pages primarily to manipulate rankings can qualify as scaled content abuse. Affiliate sites can add value when they provide useful decision support, comparisons, original information, testing, ratings, or navigation. Stage 5 is therefore controlled commercial expansion, not mass AI publishing.

## Gates
### Gate A — audience signal
- Search impressions and/or real referral traffic are measurable.
- At least one topic cluster shows sustained user interest.

### Gate B — commercial signal
- Affiliate clicks are measurable.
- At least one article or topic cluster demonstrates meaningful commercial intent.

### Gate C — revenue signal
- Amazon reporting shows qualifying orders/dispatched items and earnings.
- A winning cluster can be identified from external reporting data.

If Gate C is not met, improve acquisition and content quality rather than multiplying output.

## What scales
1. Winning topic clusters.
2. Closely related buyer-intent queries.
3. Product comparisons where the catalogue supports both products.
4. Supporting articles that answer purchase-decision questions.
5. Useful internal links between related commercial content.
6. Publishing frequency, in bounded increments.

## Current catalogue clusters
- Audio: microphones and headphones.
- Webcams: home-office video calls.
- Storage: portable SSDs and backup/work storage.
- Power: high-capacity laptop power banks.
- Workspace: Stream Deck productivity workflows.

Expansion should first deepen a cluster that produces evidence rather than adding unrelated categories.

## Expansion pattern
For a winning product/category:
1. Core buyer guide.
2. Specific use-case guide.
3. Direct comparison with another catalogue-supported product.
4. Alternative/upgrade decision guide when the catalogue supports it.
5. Problem-solving article tied to the same purchase intent.

Every new article must have a distinct user purpose. Do not create near-duplicate pages by changing wording around the same keyword.

## Production controls
- Keep the catalogue as the commercial source of truth.
- Keep affiliate URLs exact and catalogue-approved.
- Keep publication validation blocking on affiliate mismatches.
- Preserve source-bound product facts.
- Do not invent tests, prices, reviews, awards, compatibility, rankings, or specifications.
- Do not create pages solely to capture search queries.
- Keep authorship/editorial responsibility clear.
- Keep internal linking useful to readers rather than manufactured for SEO.
- Keep publishing volume bounded until external performance data justifies an increase.

## Scaling increments
- Baseline: current scheduled production.
- Expansion 1: add a small number of articles around the strongest cluster.
- Expansion 2: increase frequency only if traffic and affiliate-click quality remain healthy.
- Expansion 3: add another cluster only after the first cluster demonstrates repeatable commercial performance.

Never jump directly from one daily article to mass publishing.

## Decision matrix
| Evidence | Action |
|---|---|
| Low traffic, low clicks | Improve discovery/distribution; do not scale production. |
| Traffic, low affiliate clicks | Improve commercial relevance and article structure. |
| High affiliate clicks, no orders yet | Validate product fit and collect more data before scaling heavily. |
| Orders/earnings concentrated in one cluster | Expand that cluster first. |
| Multiple clusters producing earnings | Increase production in bounded increments. |
| Content quality deteriorates as volume rises | Reduce volume and fix the bottleneck. |

## Success definition
**winning intent → useful content → qualified traffic → affiliate clicks → purchases → earnings → more winning content**

Scale is justified by economics and evidence, not by article count.
