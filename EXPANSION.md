# Stage 7 — Expand

## Objective
Expand TechSignal only where Stage 6 evidence shows that the existing commercial model can be repeated.

**Rule: replicate proven economics before adding complexity.**

## Expansion gates
- A commercially meaningful winner has been identified.
- The winner is repeatable enough to justify additional production.
- The new opportunity serves a closely related buyer decision or a deliberately selected new market.
- Catalogue/product evidence is available before publication.
- Distribution and measurement exist for the expansion.

## Expansion order
1. Deepen the strongest existing product/topic cluster.
2. Add adjacent buyer-intent questions.
3. Add catalogue-supported comparisons and alternatives.
4. Expand into a closely related product cluster.
5. Consider another marketplace or revenue source only when operationally justified.
6. Consider a materially new vertical only after repeatability is demonstrated.

## Replication test
For every proposed expansion record:
- Existing winner being replicated.
- User problem being served.
- Products/evidence available.
- Expected commercial relationship.
- Measurement available.
- Bounded initial volume.
- Success and kill criteria.

## Anti-expansion rules
- Do not expand because the content pipeline has spare capacity.
- Do not create unrelated categories merely to increase page count.
- Do not duplicate the same article across categories or keywords.
- Do not expand into products that cannot be factually and commercially validated.
- Do not add marketplaces without the required affiliate/account setup.

## Success definition
Expansion creates a second repeatable source of qualified traffic and/or earnings without materially reducing content quality or economic efficiency.
